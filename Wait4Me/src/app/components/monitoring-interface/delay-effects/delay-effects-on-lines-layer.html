You can search for any line departing from any station at any time of day and it will be displayed on the map once its selected. By moving the slider you can see the impact any added delay will have on transfers at onward stops. The colors symbolize how much "transfer buffer time" remains with this specific delay. This buffer time is defined as the difference between arrival time of the service plus the time it takes to transfer at this stop and the earliest departure of a different service from this stop. If this difference becomes negative, the connection to that departing service is broken. Additional information about buffer times can be revealed by clicking on the stations. All this data was calculated for the 09.12.2026 (typical weekday).
